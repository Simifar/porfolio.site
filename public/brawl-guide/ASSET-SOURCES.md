# Brawl Stars guide artwork

Supercell publishes these WebP images and WebM clips in the playable-character and game-mode carousels on its public [Brawl Stars website](https://supercell.com/brawlstars/en/). The [Russian page](https://supercell.com/brawlstars/ru/) supplies class, rarity and mode labels. The full-body images are still posters for the website’s character videos. The portraits and mode icons come from the same page’s carousel controls.

The local files are byte-for-byte copies of the media at the URLs below. The guide uses them to identify and discuss Brawl Stars. The colors and platforms around them are part of the website design. SHA-256 digests identify the source bytes retrieved on 19 November 2026.

This material is unofficial and is not endorsed by Supercell. For more information see [Supercell’s Fan Content Policy](https://supercell.com/en/fan-content-policy/).

## Game references

The public [brawler carousel data](https://supercell.com/_next/static/chunks/1988.12ac106f2bcead8e.js) associates each featured brawler with their official class, rarity, portrait and poster. The English and Russian game pages above publish the objectives and names used in the guide’s three modes.

Supercell’s [Spike Star Power announcement](https://supercell.com/brawlstars/en/blog/game-updates/bull-spike-and-dynamike-star-powers-are-out/), [SpongeBob patch notes](https://supercell.com/brawlstars/en/blog/game-updates/patch-notes-spongebob-squarepants/), [Katana Kingdom release notes](https://supercell.com/brawlstars/en/blog/release-notes/updated-june-12-release-notes-the-battle-for-katana-kingdom/) and [November 2026 release notes](https://supercell.com/brawlstars/en/blog/game-updates/release-notes-november-2026/) discuss these brawlers’ abilities and changes to their moves.
